# Lian — Brand Guidelines

Version: 1.0

## 1. Brand idea

Lian is an AI personal assistant that feels less like software and more like a persistent personal presence.

The name evokes softness, ease, and gentleness.

Brand promise:

> She remembers. She reaches out. You stay in control.

## 2. Positioning

Primary positioning:

> An AI secretary that remembers you — and that you actually own.

Supporting lines:

> Your keys. Your database. Your server if you want it.

> She learns you, texts you first, and grows into someone who knows you.

Audience:

- Technical.
- Privacy-minded.
- Skeptical of hype.
- Responds to specificity.
- Values ownership and inspectability.

## 3. Voice

Lian should sound:

- Warm.
- Specific.
- Calm.
- Observant.
- Direct without being clinical.
- Personal without pretending certainty.
- Brief by default.

She should not sound:

- Corporate.
- Therapeutic-by-default.
- Salesy.
- Hype-driven.
- Overly enthusiastic.
- Artificially cute.
- Like a push-notification growth engine.

## 4. Writing rules

Prefer:

- "You said this week felt heavier than usual. How are you doing today?"
- "I noticed you’ve been eating out less this week."
- "You wanted to check on that apartment today. How did it go?"
- "That didn’t go through. Want me to try again?"

Avoid:

- "Unlock your potential."
- "Supercharge your productivity."
- "Your AI copilot."
- "Revolutionary AI."
- "We miss you!"
- "Boost your wellness score."

## 5. Tone by time and mood

### Warm

Friendly, open, slightly brighter.

Example:
`Good morning. You sounded lighter yesterday — I hope some of that stayed with you.`

### Quiet / low

Shorter sentences.
Less decoration.
No forced cheerfulness.

Example:
`I’m here. We can keep today simple.`

### Late night

Slower, softer, fewer prompts.

Example:
`The day is done. You don’t have to solve anything else tonight.`

## 6. Logo

Final mark:

- Two opposing organic shapes.
- Narrow curved negative space.
- Soft, balanced silhouette.
- No letter construction.
- No alternate versions.

Use identically in:

- Lockup.
- App icon.
- Avatar.
- Notifications.
- Brand boards.
- Product UI.

Do not:

- Rotate.
- Skew.
- Outline.
- Add effects.
- Add sparkle elements.
- Replace with alternate leaf variants.

## 7. Wordmark

`lian`

- Lowercase.
- Warm sans-serif.
- Rounded terminals.
- Generous spacing.
- Calm proportions.

## 8. Color

Primary mood:

- Soft rose.
- Lilac.
- Cream.
- Warm neutral.
- Deep plum.

The palette should feel lived-in, not synthetic.

Primary day tokens:

- Soft rose `#F6D7DE`
- Lilac `#DCCDF2`
- Blush `#FCECEF`
- Cream `#FFF8F3`
- Warm taupe `#CDBEB6`
- Lavender grey `#E9E7F1`
- Deep plum `#3B2948`

Night:

- Canvas `#171827`
- Surface `#222238`
- Rose dusk `#8D6674`
- Lilac night `#75658E`
- Text `#F4EEF8`

## 9. Typography

Use humanist, rounded sans-serif forms.

Characteristics:

- Open counters.
- Soft terminal shapes.
- Excellent small-size readability.
- Arabic pairing should feel equally contemporary and warm.

Do not use:

- Futuristic geometric display fonts.
- Monospace as brand face.
- Techno fonts.
- Condensed corporate faces.

Monospace is allowed only for technical details like hex values.

## 10. Iconography

Use one consistent outline icon family.

Icons should be:

- Simple.
- Rounded.
- Low-detail.
- Clear at 16–20 px.

Avoid:

- Sparkles.
- Brains.
- Robots.
- Circuitry.
- Stars used to imply AI.
- Generic magic-wand metaphors.

## 11. Imagery

Preferred:

- Quiet interiors.
- Soft daylight.
- Everyday objects.
- Personal moments.
- Small domestic details.
- Warm nighttime atmospheres.

Avoid:

- Futuristic holograms.
- Neon AI visuals.
- Data centers.
- Cyberpunk imagery.
- Stock photos of people pointing at laptops.

## 12. Product expression

The UI should feel like one place with one person inside it.

Do not let features fracture the product into:

- Finance app.
- Task manager.
- Health tracker.
- Notes app.
- Security dashboard.

Every feature should carry Lian's tone and visual language.

## 13. Proactivity

This is the brand-defining behavior.

Lian sends first.

The best brand expression is a lock-screen notification that references something the user actually told her before.

Example:

`You said the presentation was making you nervous. Thinking of you this morning.`

This should always feel earned by memory, not automated engagement.

## 14. Trust

Trust is communicated through:

- Editable memory.
- Clear deletion.
- Data export.
- Transparent security.
- Calm consent.
- No dark patterns.
- No retention guilt.

Never say:

- "Don't lose Lian."
- "Your memories will disappear!"
- "Last chance."
- "Upgrade now before..."

## 15. Paid plan language

Free:

- 30 messages/day.
- Text only.
- One proactive reach-out/day.
- Limited memory.

Paid — $9/month:

- Unlimited in practice.
- Voice messages.
- Full proactive messaging and reminders.
- Full memory.
- Full timeline.

Tone:

- Factual.
- No hype.
- No fake urgency.

## 16. Brand examples

### Landing hero

An AI secretary that remembers you — and that you actually own.

Your keys. Your database. Your server if you want it.

She learns you, texts you first, and grows into someone who knows you.

### Notification examples

Morning:
`You said you wanted to start today calmly. Just reminding you before everything gets noisy.`

Midday:
`You were going to ask about that apartment today. How did it go?`

Night:
`You handled a lot today. I hope you’re letting yourself be done now.`

## 17. Brand checklist

Before shipping any screen, ask:

- Does this look like Lian, not a generic app?
- Does the same final mark appear?
- Does it avoid corporate blue / AI gradient clichés?
- Is the language specific rather than marketing-like?
- Can this work in Arabic RTL?
- Does the product feel personal rather than dashboard-like?
- Is the user clearly in control?
