Lian · theme × direction
Three themes, two directions, one hierarchy.
Time controls luminance and environment. Mood controls accent temperature, chroma, density and motion. When they disagree, time wins the canvas and mood wins the accents — so warm at 2am keeps the night canvas with warmer rose and lilac, and quiet at night keeps the night canvas with the quiet palette's restraint and less decoration. Information architecture never changes; only the surface does.

Rules that hold in every combination
Body text is 16px and every text/background pair meets AA — 4.5:1 normal, 3:1 large. Secondary text uses the muted token or darker, never taupe, rose or lilac. Transitions are 160–220ms for UI and 250–400ms when the surface mood shifts. Low-amplitude opacity and background only; no ambient loops. Colour is never the only state signal — the active tab carries a tint, a heavier label and a filled icon. Gradients appear only as low-contrast environmental surfaces. Never as an "AI effect", and never purple-to-blue. Information architecture is identical across all six combinations. The theme changes warmth, contrast and decoration — nothing structural.
